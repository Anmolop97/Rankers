3 Step 3: Stefan's Law Logarithmic Formula & Intercept P = εSσTⁿ
1. Stefan's Law & Logarithmic Form
log₁₀(P) = log₁₀(εSσ) + n × log₁₀(T)
⇒ log₁₀(εSσ) = log₁₀(P) - n × log₁₀(T)
2. Reference Point Chosen on Line
Point 7: Log₁₀(T) = 3.30  •  Log₁₀(P) = 0.18
3. Numerical Substitution
log₁₀(εSσ) = 0.18 - (3.42 × 3.30)
4. Intermediate Calculation
log₁₀(εSσ) = 0.18 - 11.2860
Final Output (Neeche)
log₁₀(εSσ) = -11.1060 εSσ = 10^(-11.1060)
4 Step 4: Stefan-Boltzmann Constant (σ) σ = 10^c / (εS)
1. Mathematical Formula
σ = 10^[log₁₀(εSσ)] / (ε × S)
2. Values Put Into Formula
10^c = 10^(-11.1060) = 7.8343 × 10⁻¹² W  •  ε·S = 1.4585 × 10⁻⁴ m²
3. Numerical Substitution
σ = 10^(-11.1060) / (1.4585 × 10⁻⁴)
4. Intermediate Calculation
σ = (7.8343 × 10⁻¹²) / (1.4585 × 10⁻⁴)
Final Output (Neeche)
σ = 5.37 × 10⁻⁸ W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴ Lab Experimental Result
