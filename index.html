1 Cathode Surface Area (S) & Emissivity (ε) S = 2πrl
Radius of cathode, r = 0.31 × 10⁻² m
Length of cathode, l = 3.12 × 10⁻² m
Emissivity of nickel cathode, ε = 0.24
Surface Area, S = 2 × π × r × l
S = 2 × 3.1416 × (0.31 × 10⁻²) × (3.12 × 10⁻²)
S = 6.08 × 10⁻⁴ m²
Effective Radiation Factor:
ε · S = 0.24 × (6.08 × 10⁻⁴) = 1.46 × 10⁻⁴ m²
2 Slope of Graph II (n) n = Δy / Δx
From the straight-line graph of log₁₀(P) vs log₁₀(T), taking two points on the line:
Point A: (x₁, y₁) = (2.80, -1.51)  •  Point B: (x₂, y₂) = (3.40, 0.54)
Slope, n = (y₂ - y₁) / (x₂ - x₁)
n = [0.54 - (-1.51)] / [3.40 - 2.80]
n = 2.05 / 0.60
n = 3.42
3 Determination of Stefan-Boltzmann Constant (σ) P = εSσTⁿ
According to Stefan's Law: P = ε S σ Tⁿ
Taking log on both sides:
log₁₀(P) = log₁₀(ε S σ) + n · log₁₀(T)
log₁₀(ε S σ) = log₁₀(P) - n · log₁₀(T)
Choosing reference point from graph line: (log₁₀ T = 3.30, log₁₀ P = 0.18)
log₁₀(εSσ) = 0.18 - (3.4167 × 3.30)
log₁₀(εSσ) = 0.18 - 11.275 = -11.095
Taking Antilog:
ε S σ = 10⁻¹¹·⁰⁹⁵ = 8.03 × 10⁻¹² W
Now, σ = (ε S σ) / (ε S)
σ = (8.03 × 10⁻¹²) / (1.46 × 10⁻⁴)
σ = 5.51 × 10⁻⁸ W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴
4 Percentage Error σ standard = 5.67 × 10⁻⁸
Standard value of σ = 5.67 × 10⁻⁸ W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴
Percentage Error = [ |Calculated σ - Standard σ| / Standard σ ] × 100%
= [ |5.51 × 10⁻⁸ - 5.67 × 10⁻⁸| / (5.67 × 10⁻⁸) ] × 100%
= [ 0.16 / 5.67 ] × 100%
= 2.8%
RESULTS:
1. Slope of Graph II (n) = 3.42
2. Stefan-Boltzmann Constant (σ) = 5.51 × 10⁻⁸ W·m⁻²·K⁻⁴
3. Percentage Error = 2.8%
